# AXON Canon — Corrections

Three amendments, addressing exactly the three weaknesses the Reality Report found. Nothing else in canon changes. Where an existing word already does the job, it's reused rather than replaced — one new verb is added because no existing word covered it; everything else is an explicit widening of what already exists, never a new concept layered on top.

---

## Correction 1 — The Real Human Problem

**What was wrong.** "I have to keep re-explaining myself to AI" is a real wound, but it's narrower than it needs to be — it only resonates with someone who already has a tiring relationship with chatbots. It says nothing to a non-AI user, and it undersells what professionals, creators, and builders actually lose.

**The deeper problem, underneath all five audiences:**

**You are the only thing that has to keep remembering you.**

An AI user feels this as re-explaining themselves to a machine. A non-AI user feels it every time they repeat their account number to the third customer service department, or re-tell a story to someone who should already know it. A professional feels it every time they're the sole continuity between two systems that don't talk to each other. A creator feels it reconstructing, months later, why an idea mattered — the excitement gone, only the note left. A builder feels it opening code they wrote themselves and no longer remembering why. None of these are AI problems. AI just happens to be the newest, most irritating place the same old tax shows up.

**What this changes, precisely:** the *positioning* — the sentence AXON uses to describe the wound in copy, in pitches, in the site's opening claim — is now the universal one above, not the AI-specific one. **The film's cold open does not change.** "As I mentioned yesterday..." to a machine remains the correct dramatization for the specific audience that scene is built to move — it's simply understood, now, as one true instance of the deeper wound, not the whole of it. The scene was never wrong. It was just standing in, unlabeled, for something bigger than itself.

*Amends: AXON_MEMORY_Creative_Direction.md, Section 1, item 1 — clarification only, no change to the redesigned story in Section 2.*

## Correction 2 — Trust, Ownership and Deletion

**What was wrong.** "Retire, not delete" has no real deletion path behind it, no stated boundary between AXON's own products sharing a store, no answer for who owns a Thread built at work, and an explicit philosophical stance against export that reads as lock-in outside the metaphor.

**Deletion.** Retire remains the default, automatic behavior — nothing is lost simply because it stopped being active. But a person can always choose the opposite, deliberately: **Delete** — one new verb, the tenth, meaning permanent, irreversible removal, on request, honored completely, every time. Retiring is what happens by default. Deleting is what a person chooses, on purpose, and it always wins.

**Ownership.** A Thread belongs to the person who built it, structurally as well as legally. At work, anything a person built individually stays theirs when they leave — unless it was explicitly, separately marked as shared team memory at the moment it was created. Nothing becomes shared by default just because it happened during work.

**Privacy — the facet boundary.** "One store, many facets" describes where memory lives, not who can see it. By default, a Thread is visible wherever it's relevant, which is the entire point of the promise. But exactly like Anchor already lets someone deliberately override the system's own computed weight, a person can deliberately bound a Thread to one facet only — a felt, conscious act, not an automatic one. A sensitive financial thread doesn't have to rely on probability to stay out of a casual conversation; it can be told to, directly.

**Export.** The refusal to be "a file" describes a Thread's *living* shape inside AXON — its position, its weight, its connections — and that stays true; none of that is file-shaped, and it shouldn't become file-shaped just to be portable. But the living shape and the right to a copy are two different things. Whatever a Thread looks like while it's alive inside AXON, a complete, plain copy of everything held about a person can always be produced, in full, on request. Owning something and being able to leave with a copy of it were never in conflict — only the metaphor made them sound like they were.

**Import — resolved without a new concept.** A person's existing history from somewhere else isn't a special operation and doesn't need a new word. It's Capture, the exact verb already defined for the moment a single memory is kept — just applied to many memories at once, from an old export file instead of a single sentence typed today. Bringing in six months of context from elsewhere is Capture at a different scale, not a new mechanic bolted on beside it.

*Amends: AXON_Memory_Native_Product_Language.md, Section 2 ("Retire") and Section 6 ("The Ecosystem"); adds Delete to AXON_Lexicon.md Section 3 (Verbs) and the final reference list.*

## Correction 3 — AXON Beyond Its Own Apps

**What was wrong.** "One memory, everywhere" currently means everywhere *inside AXON* — three products, not the tools a real life is actually built on. That gap is the fastest thing a skeptical or experienced visitor finds.

**The resolution uses the chip system already built to hold it.** The Glass App-Chip System was already designed to scale to "whatever comes after" — that clause simply hasn't been used yet. The row of chips doesn't stop at Memory, Companion, and Trading OS. Any tool a person already lives in — a folder of files, an existing AI chat history, a calendar, an inbox — can become its own chip in the same glass family, its own accent color, no new visual language required, because the system was already generous enough to hold this.

**No seventh word.** Extending AXON's reach outward was deliberately not given a new proprietary noun — the six core words already fixed in the Lexicon do not become seven. What widens is the *scope* of two verbs that already exist, not the vocabulary itself:

- **Capture** already means the moment something significant is kept. It now simply happens wherever significance occurs — inside a file, inside another tool, inside an old conversation someone brings with them — not only inside an AXON surface.
- **Recall** already means something resurfacing, unprompted, at a transition. It now simply surfaces wherever a person currently is — inside whatever tool they're using at that transition — not only inside an AXON product.

**What this is not.** Not a features grid of integrations, not a settings page of toggles, not a claim that AXON lives inside every app that exists on day one. It's a stated direction — the chip row grows as a person's actual life gets folded in, one tool at a time, each new chip cut from the same glass as the ones before it. "Everywhere" stops being a word doing more work than the product, and becomes a description of where the chip row is currently allowed to grow, honestly scoped to wherever it's actually reached.

*Amends: AXON_Physical_Motion_Design_Language.md, Section 6 ("Glass App-Chip System") — the app-object list is explicitly reopened; no rule in that section changes, only its stated membership.*

## What did not change

The six-beat film. The Core as a living mineral object. Thread, Weight, Depth, Facet as the four structural nouns. The unbroken-line mark and how it was derived. The sonic arc and its one rule. Retire as the default lifecycle behavior. One store, many facets, as the architecture. The six Lexicon nouns, still six. Every Experience and Product Principle not named above. Nothing here was added because it would look good. Everything here was added because a real person, encountering AXON honestly, would have stopped at exactly this point — and now there's a plain answer waiting at each one, built entirely from words the canon already had.
